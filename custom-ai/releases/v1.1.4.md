Fixes the main-menu game-entry requirement introduced in full release build **26003210** (Steam app **4954910**).

With custom AI enabled and saved, **Play** works without browser sign-in to the game's AI service. The Steam client must still be initialized and logged in for lobbies. Turning custom AI off restores the original game-service sign-in checks.

The menu removes game-service sign-in recovery messages while showing custom-provider status. Game-service authentication, credentials, and network requests are preserved.

Close the game, extract the complete ZIP into its folder, and run **Install Custom AI.cmd**. Existing settings, sign-in sessions, and original-assembly backups are preserved. Open F8 and click **Save Settings** after enabling your provider.

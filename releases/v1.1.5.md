Adds automatic detection of the **Scam With Your Friends full release**.

## What's changed

- Detects Steam app **4954910**, normally installed in `steamapps/common/Scam With Your Friends`.
- Reads Steam's installation manifest across your Steam libraries, including installations on another drive.
- Switches a saved Playtest folder to the full release when it is installed. The old folder's mod version is cleared so it cannot incorrectly report that the new installation is already up to date.
- Updates the folder picker and setup instructions for the full release.

## How to update

1. Close the game and the AI Settings panel.
2. Run **SwyfInstaller-Setup-v1.1.5-win-x64.exe**, or let the existing app update itself.
3. Check that the selected folder is **Scam With Your Friends**, then press **Install** to apply Custom AI v1.1.3.
4. Launch the game and press **F8** to check your provider settings.

Existing settings and backups stay in their respective game folders. The setup includes the installer app; it downloads and verifies the mod separately.
